Thrive Integrated Safety & Empowerment Foundation

Creating safety. Restoring dignity. Empowering futures.

TISEF prevents family harm and human trafficking, protects and supports survivors, and builds pathways to independence for women, children, and youth — working alongside communities, government, and law enforcement.

Our work centres on violence prevention and survivor support — with a focus on family harm and human trafficking — driven by lived-experience insight, community engagement, and peer-reviewed research.

A holistic model, built for the whole journey

TISEF supports vulnerable women, children, and survivors of violence through prevention initiatives, direct protection services, legal advocacy, and economic empowerment. We address root causes — poverty, gender inequity, and lack of access to education — not just individual incidents.

Four stages, one continuum of care

Every survivor's path is different, but our response follows the same four stages — from stopping harm before it starts to standing beside someone long after the crisis has passed. Choose a stage to see the programs behind it.

Stop harm before it happens

Community education, school outreach, and public sensitisation on family harm, exploitation, and trafficking risks.

Prevention of family harm & trafficking

Community education campaigns, school and youth outreach, and public sensitisation through media, training, and workshops.

Community capacity building

Training for community workers, teachers, tribal leaders, and healthcare providers to identify and respond to abuse.

Research, monitoring & evaluation

Data collection on trends in violence and trafficking, published reports, and evaluation of intervention impact.

Keep survivors safe and supported

Emergency response, safe shelter, psychosocial and mental health support, and basic needs for survivors.

Survivor protection & support services

Emergency response, safe shelter, psychosocial and mental health support, and provision of food, clothing, and basic needs.

Anti-trafficking interventions

Identification and rescue in collaboration with agencies, case management, and rehabilitation through education, housing, and training.

Networking & partnerships

Coordinated response with government agencies, law enforcement, NGOs, and international bodies, plus referral networks for comprehensive care.

Pursue justice, hold systems accountable

Legal aid, court accompaniment, and advocacy for stronger enforcement of protective laws.

Legal support & access to justice

Legal aid, court accompaniment, and advocacy for stronger enforcement of laws on violence and trafficking.

Advocacy & policy engagement

Campaigns to influence national and local policy, and partnerships with government and international organisations.

Build independence that lasts

Vocational training, microfinance, and education sponsorships that build lasting independence.

Women & youth empowerment

Skills acquisition, vocational training, microfinance, and education sponsorships for vulnerable children and youth.
